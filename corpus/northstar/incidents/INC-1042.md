# INC-1042 — Elevated checkout latency and capture failures

- **Status:** resolved
- **Severity:** SEV-1
- **Opened:** 2026-03-12T14:05:00Z
- **Resolved:** 2026-03-12T16:40:00Z

## Summary

Merchants on checkout-web saw timeouts. Capture success rate on payments-api
dropped from 99.7% to 82% for ~90 minutes.

## Cause

Root cause was saturation on **ledger-db** (connection pool exhaustion)
propagating through **ledger-service** into **payments-api**.

## Impact

- **Brightline Retail** — failed checkouts and duplicate-submit risk.
- **payments-api** — elevated p99 latency; capture endpoint errors.

## Mitigation

Followed runbook **payments-latency** (pool bump + traffic shed on
non-critical reads). Full ledger consistency check used **ledger-replay**.

## Follow-ups

- Add pool metrics to the payments-api dashboard.
- Page Mira Chen (payments-api on-call) automatically when capture error
  rate > 5% for 5 minutes.
