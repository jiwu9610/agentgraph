# INC-1099 — False soft-declines on risk-engine

- **Status:** resolved
- **Severity:** SEV-2
- **Opened:** 2026-04-02T09:10:00Z
- **Resolved:** 2026-04-02T11:00:00Z

## Summary

risk-engine began soft-declining legitimate charges after a bad feature flag
push to **risk-redis**.

## Cause

Stale risk scores in **risk-redis** after a partial flag deploy. **risk-engine**
treated missing keys as "high risk."

## Impact

- **Harbor Subscriptions** — elevated decline rate on renewals.
- **risk-engine** — incorrect scores for ~110 minutes.

## Mitigation

Rolled back the flag, flushed the bad keyspace in risk-redis, and watched
decline rate return to baseline. Priya Nair owned the response (risk-engine
owner).
